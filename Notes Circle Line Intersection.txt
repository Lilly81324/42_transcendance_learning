r = length(x * V + SV - C)

root(x*x + y*y)

We can avoid the expensive square root operation by squaring all

r^2 = length(x * V + SV - C)^2

There are at least 2 types of quadratic formulas, the normalised and the common one.
Common:
a*x^2 + b*x + c = 0
Normalised:
x^2 + p*x + q = 0
We have the first and need to turn it into the latter one.
With our previous equation we have
a = V*V
b = 2*V*D
c = D*D - r*r
Divide by a to get the normalised pq form
0 = xx + x(2VD)/(VV) + (DD - rr)/VV
Now we can get our p and q and use the pq formula
p = (2VD)/(VV)
q = (DD - rr)/VV
Remember the pq formula song!
x = -p/2 +/- root(p/2 - q)
